About This Visualization

The purpose of this visualization is to see the trend in four different crime types (burglary, motor vehicle theft, robbery, and theft) before, during, and after the COVID-19 pandemic. The data displays the total number of incidents per month, spanning a five-year period: two years before, one year during, and two years after the pandemic. Use the filters on the left to toggle specific crime types on and off. Hover over the data points on the graph to view the exact number of incidents for a given period.
