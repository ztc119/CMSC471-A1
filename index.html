Average Monthly Crime Counts by Crime Type

About This Visualization

This visualization shows monthly crime statistics for three crime types: theft, robbery, and burglary. The blue line traces the average number of crimes per month over 2015-2025. Black circles show the average monthly values. The additional points show the highest and lowest monthly counts recorded across the years 2015-2025. Hover over points to see the exact values and the year when the maximum or minimum occurred.
